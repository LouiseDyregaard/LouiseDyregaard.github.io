Gaia Follow-Up Programme

The Gaia Follow-Up Programme (GFOP) is a community driven effort to coordinate follow-up observations of Gaia exoplanet and brown dwarf detections. The programme is loosely based on the success of the TESS Follow-up Program (TFOP), but will have significant differences especially concerning publication policies. The GFOP aims to avoid duplication of observational efforts while fostering collaboration within the astronomical community.
