Gaia Follow-Up Programme

The Gaia Follow-Up Programme (GFOP) is a community driven effort to coordinate follow-up observations of Gaia exoplanet and brown dwarf detections. The GFOP aims to avoid duplication of observational efforts while fostering collaboration within the astronomical community. The programme is loosely based on the success of the TESS Follow-up Program (TFOP) with some differences that will be discussed during the kick-off event.

Kick-off meeting

October 20, 2026

The GFOP will be kick-started in a fully online meeting a bit more than a month before Gaia DR4. This will be an important opportunity for the community to discuss the goals and structure of the GFOP, as well as to address any questions from potential participants. Since GFOP is fully community driven, the success of the programme relies on the participation of the research groups interested in Gaia DR4 follow-up.

To participate you must register in advance and/or join the GFOP email list where the latest updates will be shared. To join, or recieve the registration link, please email the organising committee via this link.
